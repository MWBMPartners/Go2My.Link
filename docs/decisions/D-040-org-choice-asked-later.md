# D-040 — The account is erased on time; an unanswered organisation choice is asked for by e-mail

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Issue:** #247
- **Question:** If a person became the only member after asking for erasure, what happens to the organisation's details?
- **Answer:** They are asked. The owner chose "pause and ask, but remove if no answer after a period set by a global administrator (default 30 days)", and then: erase the account on time, and let only the organisation's details wait. The person is e-mailed a one-off link to choose; with no answer, the details are removed.
- **Rules out:** Delaying the erasure of the personal account; removing or keeping without asking.
- **Where it is implemented:** Not yet built.
- **Supersedes:** nothing. Adds to D-031
