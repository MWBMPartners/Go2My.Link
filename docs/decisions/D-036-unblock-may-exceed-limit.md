# D-036 — Removing a staff block may take a person over the limit

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Issue:** #327
- **Question:** May removing a staff block leave a person with more than 50 active links?
- **Answer:** Yes. Staff actions are never blocked; the person cannot add more until back under 50.
- **Rules out:** Leaving a restored link off because of the limit.
- **Where it is implemented:** As built in #327.
- **Supersedes:** nothing. Adds to D-024 and D-026
