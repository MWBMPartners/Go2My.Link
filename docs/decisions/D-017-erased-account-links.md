# D-017 — Erasing an account switches its links off unless the person chooses otherwise

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Issue:** #246
- **Question:** What happens to a person's short links when they delete their account?
- **Answer:** By default they are switched off. The person may choose to keep them working for a set period, or for ever.
- **Rules out:** Switching links off with no choice; keeping them working by default.
