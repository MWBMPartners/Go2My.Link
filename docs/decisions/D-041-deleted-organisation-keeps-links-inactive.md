# D-041 — A deleted organisation keeps its links and QR codes, marked inactive

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Issue:** #247
- **Question:** When an organisation is deleted, what happens to its links and the QR codes connected to them?
- **Answer:** The owner said: "On deletion of an organisation, kept their links, and or qr codes (CueRCode project) but flag them as inactive". Asked how this meets the keep-working choice: "Keep their period". In full: Keep its links and their QR code connections (CueRCode), flagged as inactive. Where a person removing their own organisation also chose to keep links working for a period or for ever, the links stay working for that time and become inactive afterwards.
- **Rules out:** Deleting a removed organisation's links or QR connections; organisation removal overriding a chosen keep-working period.
- **Where it is implemented:** Not yet built (#247). The QR codes themselves are held by CueRCode (#276).
- **Supersedes:** nothing. Adds to D-021 and D-031
