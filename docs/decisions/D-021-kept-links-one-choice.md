# D-021 — One choice with set periods covers all of a person's links

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Issue:** #246
- **Question:** How does a person choose to keep links working when deleting their account?
- **Answer:** One choice for all their links: switch off now (default), keep 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or for ever.
- **Rules out:** A separate choice per link; picking an arbitrary date.
